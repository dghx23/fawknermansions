Terms & Conditions

Guest Wi-Fi is supplied for authorised Fawkner Mansions guests and visitors with a valid entitlement. Access may be suspended where necessary to operate, secure or maintain the service.

  • Use the service lawfully and do not interfere with other users, systems or devices.
  • Keep voucher/token details within your booking party and do not resell or publicly share access.
  • Package validity is tied to the purchased entitlement and may also end when the associated stay ends.
  • Availability and performance can vary and are not guaranteed continuously.

Technical connection, entitlement and transaction-reference information may be retained for service operation, billing, security and troubleshooting.

Fair Use Policy

Paid packages provide unlimited data during the valid entitlement period, subject to reasonable network management so that one user or device does not materially degrade service for other guests.

  • No deliberate network abuse, attacks, bulk unsolicited messaging or unlawful activity.
  • Automated or unusually heavy traffic may be rate-limited where required to protect service quality.
  • Multi-device access is permitted for devices authorised to the same guest/booking, within any configured device or concurrency limit.

Pay by card
