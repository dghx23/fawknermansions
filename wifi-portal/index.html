Terms & Conditions

Guest Wi‑Fi is provided to authorised guests and visitors with a valid booking-linked entitlement.

  • Use the service lawfully and do not interfere with other users or private hotel systems.
  • Do not publicly share or resell a voucher/token.
  • Access validity follows the purchased package and may also end at checkout.
  • Availability and performance may vary.

Fair Use Policy

Packages provide unlimited data for the valid entitlement period, subject to reasonable network management to protect service quality.

  • Multi-device access is permitted within the booking’s configured device limit.
  • Abusive, unlawful or automated traffic may be restricted.
  • Technical session and entitlement information may be logged for billing, security and support.
