Unverified working assumption

Start with ~27 theoretical bedrooms: 9 × 3 levels

The current planning assumption is approximately nine bedrooms on each of three accommodation levels. This is not yet an onsite verified room schedule. Historical sources that mention 27 flats concern an earlier building configuration and should not be used as proof of today's hotel inventory.

A current-era accommodation source in the research dossier appeared to market about 15 rooms at one point, which is why the model keeps usable/sellable inventory separate from the theoretical 27-room physical estimate.

What to measure

Replace estimates onsite

Laser length × width, ceiling height, bed type, basin, window, outlets, TV, network signal, current status and room ID.

Important

Bedroom area is not building area

The model excludes corridors, shared bathrooms, kitchen, library/lounge, reception, stairs, walls and plant/service spaces.
