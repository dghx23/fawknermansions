Government/industry controls that shape the design

Conditional rule

Victorian residents register

If the accommodation provider has a relevant general or on-premises liquor licence, Victorian guidance requires a residents register recording name, address, room number and check-in/check-out dates. A digital register is permitted and must be immediately accessible onsite when requested. ID may be requested to verify details; a copy does not need to be kept.

Privacy benchmark

Collect the minimum

OAIC APP 3 guidance says collection should be reasonably necessary and proportionate, using data minimisation. “Helpful” or “convenient” alone is not enough where the APPs apply.

Retention

Delete/de-identify when no longer needed

OAIC APP 11 requires covered entities to protect information and take reasonable steps to destroy/de-identify information no longer needed, unless another law/order requires retention.

Identity verification

DVS is possible, but controlled

Approved private businesses can use the DVS for their own business purposes through an approved Gateway Service Provider. Express consent is mandatory and access must meet the government's criteria/participation agreement.

Questions that must be resolved before production

  • Operating entity: which legal entity operates Fawkner Mansions Heritage Hotel and will be the data controller/contracting party?
  • Privacy Act applicability: confirm turnover and any exception that brings the operator within Privacy Act coverage. Design to APP-level controls regardless.
  • Liquor licence: does the property hold a general/on-premises licence that triggers the Victorian residents-register requirement?
  • Insurance: what guest-record, security, cyber and access-control conditions actually appear in the current policy? Do not assume.
  • Fire/emergency obligations: identify the exact building/emergency-management requirements applicable to this property before making legal claims in the owner pitch.
  • Retention: agree operational/legal retention periods for stay records, verification results, audit logs and any incident records.
