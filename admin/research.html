New Revelations & Contradictions — v2 vs v1

Unsupported sensational claim quarantined. The supplied audit attributes “drug den”, “brothel” and “chronic squalor” language to a supposed 2016 Age article. The located “mystery building” article is published in 2019 and does not support those phrases. Do not publish them unless the original underlying source is found.
2016/2019 confusion explained. The Age article is dated 16 April 2019, but several photographs embedded in it carry file captions dated 19 August 2016.
PAO date corrected. Planning-review material traces the Punt Road widening reservation to the original 1954 planning scheme, not merely “the 1960s”.
“Oldest” wording tightened. Official Port Phillip heritage analysis identifies Melbourne Mansions (1906, demolished) as earlier, and describes Fawkner Mansions as the first purpose-built flats outside the city and the oldest surviving block of flats in Melbourne.
Review comparison corrected. Tripadvisor’s 1.0 score currently rests on one review, while Booking.com has hundreds. The raw scores must not be compared as if they have equal evidentiary weight.
Date/configuration conflicts remain open. 1909–10/1910/1912 and 25/27 are not resolved by the current online record; original permits, plans, rate books and early advertisements are the next step.

Research method & version control

Evidence classes

Verified primary · official secondary · reputable media · attributed owner/operator statement · marketing claim · dated review snapshot · user-supplied material · anecdote · inference · unresolved.

Immutable runs

Each substantial pass is stored as research/runs/vN.md. Later runs correct earlier ones without deleting the audit trail.

Mandatory diff

Every new run must state what is genuinely new, what contradicts earlier work, and which earlier wording should be corrected or softened.

Image governance

Creator, source, date, transformation status, rights owner and permission are required before an image graduates from research material to public publication.
