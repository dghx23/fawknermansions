Why the building matters

The safest current heritage wording is deliberately precise rather than promotional.

Among Victoria’s earliest surviving purpose-built flats

The Victorian heritage record identifies Fawkner Mansions as the oldest residential flats in the City of Stonnington and one of the oldest surviving examples in Victoria. Its exact construction dating still varies slightly between sources, so the research system preserves that contradiction rather than hiding it.

A building whose social role kept changing

Its history moves from experimental early flat living to professional households, Alfred Hospital nurses’ accommodation, low-cost rooming-house use, deterioration and eventual re-emergence as a heritage-branded accommodation business.

A century of changing use

The project treats these as phases in one continuous building history rather than isolated anecdotes.

The current operating model

Working assumptions are kept editable until an onsite survey replaces them with measured facts.

Room inventory model

Current planning assumption: roughly 27 theoretical bedrooms based on approximately nine rooms across three levels. Sellable inventory is modelled separately and defaults conservatively until a room-by-room count is completed.

Open room model

Guest Wi‑Fi + check-in

Optional guest Wi‑Fi can be purchased during digital check-in, with the resulting entitlement linked to the guest, booking and room. Fixed building equipment—TVs, cameras and sensors—remains permanently connected on isolated managed networks.
