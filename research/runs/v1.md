# Deep Research Run v1 — Initial media and heritage synthesis
**Date:** 25 September 2026  
**Status:** superseded by v2, retained for audit trail

## Scope
Initial broad online research into Fawkner Mansions, 250 Punt Road, Prahran, covering heritage status, construction history, Alfred Hospital use, rooming-house/homelessness use, the Punt Road Public Acquisition Overlay, modern hotel operation and media coverage.

## Core findings at v1
1. Fawkner Mansions is a very early purpose-built block of flats at 250 Punt Road, Prahran, designed by Ernest H. Willis. Architectural sources commonly date it to 1910.
2. The safest “oldest” formulation identified was that Melbourne Mansions (Collins Street, 1906, demolished) preceded it; Fawkner Mansions is described in municipal heritage comparative analysis as the oldest surviving block of flats in Melbourne.
3. The building became Alfred Hospital nurses’ accommodation. Victorian Auditor-General reporting records accommodation for 80 nursing staff and closure in October 1987 because of an annual operating deficit of about $90,000.
4. By the late 1990s–2013 it was being used as low-cost rooming-house accommodation, including referrals for people facing homelessness. HomeGround coordinated the rehousing of 45 residents when it closed in 2013.
5. The building is protected by Stonnington Heritage Overlay HO448 and is **not** presently on the Victorian Heritage Register.
6. A long-standing Public Acquisition Overlay affecting the east side of Punt Road complicates investment and redevelopment. The overlay dates to the 1954 planning scheme.
7. By the 2020s the building had reopened as Fawkner Mansions Heritage Hotel.
