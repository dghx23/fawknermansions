Fawkner Mansions, Punt Road, Prahran
116 years of living history — captured, priceless

Fawkner Mansions, at 250 Punt Road, Prahran, designed by Ernest H. Willis and constructed in 1910–11, is the oldest residential flats in the City of Stonnington and one of the oldest in Victoria. A fine and highly intact example of Edwardian Free Classical architecture, Fawkner Mansions remains a distinctive part of Melbourne’s built heritage.

Fawkner Mansions

On the corner of Punt and Commercial roads in Prahran stands the oldest surviving block of flats in the City of Stonnington — and one of the oldest in Victoria. This book traces its Edwardian Free Classical architecture, a century of residents, and a present-day resident’s own research and digital preservation of a building whose future is not guaranteed.
