Fawkner Mansions

250 Punt Road, Prahran, Victoria · Heritage Overlay HO448
A proposal for the research, digital preservation, and public documentation of a heritage-listed building — and what it can mean for the people who own it.
Prepared by David Hough for Dr Jeff Nguyen · September 2026

1. The opportunity

Fawkner Mansions is, on the current heritage record, the oldest surviving residential flats in the City of Stonnington, and one of the oldest in Victoria.

Designed by Ernest H. Willis and built in 1910–11, the building has stood for 116 years with almost no public documentation beyond its council heritage citation (Heritage Overlay HO448). This proposal is an offer to change that — at no financial cost to the owners — in exchange for a modest, defined rent concession during the research period.

2. The value I bring

An evidence-trained researcher already living in the building is offering to do, essentially for free, work that would otherwise cost the owners real money to commission: professional-grade digital preservation, archival research, and a public-facing history — with an audit-style discipline applied to make sure every claim is sourced and defensible. On top of the core research and 3D/VR capture, I bring drone piloting and 3D mapping (photogrammetry) skills directly, plus documentary film-making, so the same capture work produces both the preservation record and a short history documentary at no extra cost.

What a drone-captured 3D model and VR record actually means

In plain terms: a drone photographs the building from hundreds of angles; software stitches those photos into an exact 3D digital replica; that replica can then be explored in virtual reality — putting on a headset (or using a web browser) and looking around as if physically standing inside or outside the building. If the building is ever altered or demolished, this record still exists, permanently, independent of the physical structure.

3. How this creates a legacy

This project turns Fawkner Mansions' rare heritage status into something permanent: a properly researched book, and a full digital VR record that heritage bodies, historians, and the public can reference indefinitely. Doing this now, while the building still stands largely intact, is a meaningfully time-sensitive opportunity. A well-produced documentary and a genuine "heritage building saved digitally" story is also a plausible media hook — local and potentially international press coverage that promotes the book, the documentary, and Fawkner Mansions itself simultaneously.

Beyond self-publishing, there is a real path to reach academic and publishing networks in Australia, Europe, the USA, and further afield — heritage and local-history titles are a genuine niche for university presses and specialist publishers, and university interest (see §6) makes a co-publishing or distribution conversation plausible, not just individual library sales.

6. Public-interest distribution potential

Victoria has on the order of ~2,250 schools (government, Catholic, independent — approximate, for pitch purposes) and eight Victoria-headquartered universities, each with library budgets and relevant architecture/heritage departments. Because the trade edition is print-on-demand, these are standing, ongoing orders, not a one-off run. Draft German, French, Spanish, and Chinese editions extend this to international schools and diaspora/heritage audiences.

8. Guest connectivity & digital operations

The Wi-Fi and guest-operations concepts now sit in their own workstreams rather than being compressed into the heritage pitch. The proposed guest Wi-Fi packages are A$6 for 1 night, A$10 for 2 nights, A$13 for 3 nights, A$22 for 7 days, and A$55 for 1 month, with multi-device access linked to the guest booking.

The operating concept is broader than a hotspot: managed Starlink/business backhaul, segregated internal networks, permanent connectivity for TVs/cameras/sensors, a guest-only captive portal, digital check-in, integrated EFTPOS payment, and an owner/admin backend. A separate room-and-capacity model currently uses about 27 theoretical bedrooms (9 × 3 levels) while keeping usable/sellable inventory adjustable until an onsite survey is completed.
